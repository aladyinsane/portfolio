## Problem

A state Medicaid agency needed annual supplemental payment calculations that
could withstand CMS review while accounting for changing provider participation,
commercial reimbursement patterns, cost report updates, and regulatory
requirements.

## Approach

Built provider-level payment models using commercial-to-Medicare ratio
calculations, hospital financial data, cost reports, and claims experience.
Supported annual program updates, State Plan Amendment documentation, payment
validation, and provider onboarding, backed by data validation, methodology
documentation, and assumption testing to keep the program consistent and
defensible year over year.

## Outcome

Supported a supplemental payment program serving dozens of participating
hospitals, maintaining the annual calculation methodology the program depends on
to keep operating. Built a repeatable, provider-level modeling process that
absorbs newly participating providers and annual data updates without a
methodology redesign each cycle, reducing implementation risk and keeping
payment operations continuous.
