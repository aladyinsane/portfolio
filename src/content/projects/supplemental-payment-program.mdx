## Problem

A state Medicaid agency needed annual supplemental payment calculations and
Upper Payment Limit (UPL) demonstrations that could withstand CMS review while
accounting for changing provider participation, commercial reimbursement
patterns, cost report updates, and regulatory requirements.

## Approach

Designed and maintained provider-level payment models using commercial-to-Medicare
ratio calculations, hospital financial data, cost reports, and claims experience,
covering both hospital and ground ambulance provider populations. Built the UPL
demonstrations required to justify the payments to CMS, and supported annual
program updates, State Plan Amendment documentation, payment validation, and
provider onboarding, backed by data validation, methodology documentation, and
assumption testing to keep the program consistent and defensible year over year.

## Outcome

Designed and maintained supplemental payment programs and UPL demonstrations
supporting more than $95 million in annual supplemental payments: approximately
$91 million for hospitals and $4 million for ground ambulance providers. Over
6 years, these programs have generated approximately $130 million in federal
funding. Built a repeatable, provider-level modeling process that absorbs newly
participating providers and annual data updates without a methodology redesign
each cycle, reducing implementation risk and keeping payment operations
continuous.
