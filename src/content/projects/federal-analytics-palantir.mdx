## Problem

A large US federal agency needed demographic and geographic analysis of social
drivers of health, combining public datasets with the agency's own data. A one-off
deliverable (a slide deck, a static report) wouldn't hold up as the underlying data
changed. The agency needed something that lived inside its own systems and could be
maintained after the engagement ended, not a snapshot handed off and left to go
stale.

## Approach

Led a technical team building the full pipeline, analyses, and visualizations
directly inside Palantir Foundry, the platform the agency already ran its own data
ecosystem on. That meant designing for someone else's infrastructure and constraints
from the start, not adapting a personal toolkit and hoping it transferred.

## Outcome

The product deployed directly into the agency's existing data ecosystem rather than
existing as a throwaway deliverable: built to be maintained and extended by the
agency itself, not just presented once and archived.
