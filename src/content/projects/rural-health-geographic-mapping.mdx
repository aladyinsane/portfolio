## Problem

Program stakeholders needed a defensible methodology for defining and measuring
rurality, but the alternative approaches on the table produced inconsistent
classifications and different policy implications depending on which one was
used.

## Approach

Evaluated alternative county- and census-based rurality methodologies, analyzed
population and geographic characteristics, and built interactive Power BI mapping
products so stakeholders could compare classification approaches directly and see
how each one affected program eligibility, reporting, and planning decisions.

## Outcome

Delivered interactive geographic analytics tools that let stakeholders compare
alternative rurality methodologies and understand their policy implications
before finalizing an implementation decision, converting a largely conceptual
methodology discussion into a transparent, data-driven process backed by
county-level impact analysis.
