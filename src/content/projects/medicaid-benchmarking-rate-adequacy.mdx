## Problem

A state Medicaid agency needed to know whether its reimbursement rates remained
competitive and adequate across major service categories. Existing information
wasn't enough to compare Medicaid reimbursement against Medicare, commercial
payers, and neighboring state Medicaid programs.

## Approach

Built a reimbursement benchmarking methodology using Medicaid claims, fee
schedules, Medicare payment data, commercial benchmark data, and neighboring-state
fee schedules, focused on the high-volume and high-expenditure procedure codes
that carried the most programmatic and fiscal weight. Ran service-level
reimbursement comparisons, rate adequacy analyses, budget impact modeling, and
policy research covering payment methodologies, reimbursement trends, and program
design alternatives.

## Outcome

Delivered a statewide benchmarking framework spanning six neighboring states,
Medicare, and commercial payers, built as a repeatable process that can be
refreshed annually as claims and fee schedule data change. Replaced ad hoc rate
comparisons with a structured, defensible evidence base agency leadership could
use directly in rate-setting and reimbursement policy discussions.
