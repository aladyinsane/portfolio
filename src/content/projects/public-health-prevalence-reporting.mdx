## Problem

A public-sector healthcare organization needed to compare published behavioral health
prevalence estimates across its service area and broader benchmarks. Source releases 
used different periods, geographies, estimation methods, and formats, making a direct 
trend view potentially misleading without documented selection and comparability rules.

## Approach

Built a reproducible data preparation workflow that filters and standardizes published
estimates, maps comparable measures across releases, preserves survey-period labels, 
and separates non-comparable periods rather than interpolating them. Produced both a 
portable interactive reporting model and presentation-ready comparison views, with 
validation checks for source and output schemas, keys, row counts, ranges, and 
selected value equivalence.

## Outcome

Deployed the interactive report through testing and production environments for 
stakeholder review and public access. The reporting capability brings historical, 
current, and benchmark prevalence estimates into a single documented analytical 
structure with transparent methodology, source traceability, and explicit caveats for
methodological breaks and non-comparable estimates. Stakeholder review also prompted
a follow up request for presentation-ready time-series comparisons.
