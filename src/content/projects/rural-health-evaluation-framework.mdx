## Problem

State leaders needed a way to measure the effectiveness of a large-scale rural
health transformation initiative spanning provider organizations, technology
investments, interoperability efforts, and community health improvement
activities.

## Approach

Built evaluation frameworks, performance measures, reporting strategies, rural
health dashboards, and data collection approaches, working across policy,
operational, and technical stakeholders to translate program goals into
measurable indicators and reporting requirements. Developed analytic approaches
for rural health measurement, geographic analysis, and provider performance
monitoring.

## Outcome

Established the performance measurement framework supporting implementation
oversight for a statewide rural health transformation initiative, defining the
program metrics, reporting structures, and dashboard requirements intended to
support adoption across 50 rural provider organizations and three state agencies
over the program's lifecycle: a standardized foundation for long-term program
evaluation.
